In BetterBoard, choosing what shows on a card is a toggle. Turn fields on or off and see the cards update live.

## How to show fields on cards

<Steps>
<ol>
<li>Open the card layout panel.</li>
<li>Turn on the fields you need, such as story points, due date, components, or a custom field.</li>
<li>Review the live card preview and keep the fields that help this board.</li>
</ol>
</Steps>

<NeutralCallout type="note">

**Tip:** BetterBoard reads Jira fields directly, so a multi-space board can offer custom fields from its included spaces.
