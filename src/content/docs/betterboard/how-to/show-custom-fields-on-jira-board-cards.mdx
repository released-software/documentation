In BetterBoard, choosing what shows on a card is a toggle. Turn fields on or off and see the cards update live.

## How to show fields on cards

1. Open the card layout panel.
2. Turn on the fields you need, such as story points, due date, components, or a custom field.
3. Review the live card preview and keep the fields that help this board.
