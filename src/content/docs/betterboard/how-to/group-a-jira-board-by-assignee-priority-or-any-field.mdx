BetterBoard does not hard-wire columns to status. Choose what the board groups by and change it whenever the work calls for a different view.

## How to group a board by a field

<Steps>
<ol class="betterboard-how-to-steps">
<li>
### Open grouping

Open the board's grouping setting.
</li>
<li>
### Choose a field

Choose a field such as Status, Assignee, Priority, Sprint, Labels, or a single-select custom field.
</li>
<li>
### Change the view

BetterBoard re-lays out the board immediately. Change the grouping again when you need a different view.
</li>
</ol>
</Steps>

import NeutralCallout from '../../../../components/content/NeutralCallout.astro';

<NeutralCallout type="note">

**Tip:** Grouping is a live view setting, so people can group the same board for their current task without changing the board for everyone else.

</NeutralCallout>
