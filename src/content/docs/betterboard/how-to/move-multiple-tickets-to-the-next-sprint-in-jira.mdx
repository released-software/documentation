Sprint is a field in BetterBoard, so moving tickets between sprints works like editing any other field: on the board and in bulk.

## How to move work items to a sprint

<Steps>
<ol>
<li>Hold `Shift` to select a range of cards, or hold `⌘` on Mac or `Ctrl` on Windows to select individual cards.</li>
<li>Choose the target sprint from the selection toolbar's **Sprint** field.</li>
<li>BetterBoard moves every selected work item at once.</li>
</ol>
</Steps>

See [Sprint management](/betterboard/work-faster/sprint-management/) for sprint fields and supported movements.
