A personal board across the spaces you work in stays current as new work is assigned to you. Add those spaces, then filter the board to your own work.

## How to create a My work board

1. Create a board that includes the Jira spaces you work across.
2. Open the filter bar and filter the board to your own assignments.
3. Use the resulting board to drag, group, and bulk-edit your work as it changes.
