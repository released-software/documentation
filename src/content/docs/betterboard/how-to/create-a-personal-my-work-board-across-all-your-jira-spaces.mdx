A personal board across the spaces you work in stays current as new work is assigned to you. Add those spaces, then filter the board to your own work.

## How to create a My work board

<Steps>
<ol>
<li>Create a board that includes the Jira spaces you work across.</li>
<li>Open the filter bar and filter the board to your own assignments.</li>
<li>Use the resulting board to drag, group, and bulk-edit your work as it changes.</li>
</ol>
</Steps>
