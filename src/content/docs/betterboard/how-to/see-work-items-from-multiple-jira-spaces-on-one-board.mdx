BetterBoard is built for boards that are not trapped in one space. Pick the spaces you want to see and their work appears together.

## How to show multiple spaces on one board

<Steps>
<ol class="betterboard-how-to-steps">
<li>
### Create or open a board

Create a board or open its settings.
</li>
<li>
### Select the spaces

Choose the Jira spaces the board should include.
</li>
<li>
### Shape the shared view

Group, filter, and bulk-edit work items from every selected space on the same board.
</li>
</ol>
</Steps>

import NeutralCallout from '../../../../components/content/NeutralCallout.astro';

<NeutralCallout type="note">

**Tip:** When several spaces share a board, group work by a meaningful dimension such as space, Assignee, Epic, or another field.

</NeutralCallout>
